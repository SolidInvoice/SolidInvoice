Turn invoices into payments — automatically.
============================================================

Connect a payment method once and every invoice you send gets a &quot;Pay now&quot; button. Clients pay in two clicks; the money lands in your account within days.

Invoices with online pay settle 3× faster
Average: 4 days vs. 14 days for bank-transfer-only invoices.

How it works
------------------------------------------------------------

1. Connect Stripe or PayPal in Settings → Payment methods (30 seconds).
2. Every invoice automatically shows a &quot;Pay now&quot; button.
3. Your client pays. You get notified. Status flips to Paid.

Connect a payment method: http://localhost/payments/methods
