Add your first client in under a minute.
============================================================

Clients are the foundation of everything in SolidInvoice. Once one is in your workspace, invoices, quotes, and payment history all snap into place.

What you&#039;ll need
------------------------------------------------------------
Just the essentials — you can fill in more later.

* Client name or company
* An email for sending invoices
* Default currency (we&#039;ll pick one based on your region)

Add your first client: http://localhost/clients/add
