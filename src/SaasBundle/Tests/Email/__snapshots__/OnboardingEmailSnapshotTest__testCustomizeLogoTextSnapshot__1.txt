Your logo. Your brand. Your invoices.
============================================================

Clients judge professionalism before they read a single line item. A branded invoice — your logo, your colors, your name — signals that you run a real business.

Three things to customize
------------------------------------------------------------

1. Upload your logo
   PNG, SVG, or JPG. Appears on every invoice and quote you send.

2. Pick a primary color
   Used for buttons and accents on every document you send.

3. Set your sender name
   Customize the name clients see when your invoices land in their inbox.

Brand your invoices: http://localhost/settings
