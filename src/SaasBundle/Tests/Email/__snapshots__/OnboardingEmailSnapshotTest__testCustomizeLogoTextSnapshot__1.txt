Your logo. Your colors. Your invoices.
============================================================

Branded invoices get paid faster and look unmistakably yours. Upload a logo, pick a color, and every document you send — invoices, quotes, receipts — is transformed.

Takes three minutes
------------------------------------------------------------

1. Upload your logo
   PNG, SVG, or JPG. It appears on invoices, quotes, and the client portal.

2. Pick a primary color
   Used for buttons, headings, and accents across every document you send.

3. Customise your sender email
   Send from billing@yourstudio.com — not a generic no-reply address.

Brand your workspace: http://localhost/settings
