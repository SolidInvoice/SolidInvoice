Your first invoice takes about two minutes.
============================================================

Pick your client, add a line item, hit Send. No client yet? Add one right on the invoice form. SolidInvoice handles the formatting, delivery, and tracking.

Included on every invoice, nothing extra to configure
------------------------------------------------------------
Line items, taxes, discounts, recurring schedules, and due dates — built in. Add a payment method and your client gets a 'Pay now' button too.

Create your first invoice: http://localhost/invoices/create

P.S. a draft stays a draft until you press Send. Your client only sees the invoice once it's sent.
