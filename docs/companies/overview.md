# Overview

SolidInvoice provides support for managing multiple companies.

A company can be any type of entity that needs to manage clients and send out invoices. It can be a registered business or just a personal account.

### Company Information

A company needs at least a name, and a currency set. All clients, invoices, quotes, settings etc. are unique to a company and not shared between companies. A user can create as many companies as they like, there are no limits to the amount of companies that you can create.

A user can also be invited to be part of another company, and will have acess to all the resources of every company they are invited to.
